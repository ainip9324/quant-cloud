# Zone Reversal v1

## Entry and state

- Each configured zone has `lower`, `upper`, `id`, and `effectiveFrom`.
- Before `effectiveFrom`, a zone cannot arm, trigger, flip direction, or block take profit.
- A close above `upper + width × rearmBufferRatio` arms a long-on-return state; a close below `lower - width × rearmBufferRatio` arms a short-on-return state.
- An armed zone enters only when its midpoint `(lower + upper) / 2` is inside the current K-line `[low, high]`; the planned entry price is that midpoint.
- The strategy is single-position and never adds. A rejected plan, including RR rejection, does not enter a position and leaves the zone armed for a later retry.

## Risk and exits

- Long stop: `lower - width × stopBufferRatio`; short stop: `upper + width × stopBufferRatio`.
- The raw target is risk distance multiplied by `rewardRiskRatio`. A nearer, already-effective other zone can truncate it; an already-effective disabled zone still blocks the target.
- A plan is created only when actual reward/risk is strictly greater than 1.

## Parameters and defaults

- `riskRate` (default `0.01`): fraction of equity risked per planned trade; valid `(0, 0.1]`.
- `rewardRiskRatio` (default `2.0`): raw target multiple; must be positive.
- `stopBufferRatio` (default `0.5`): zone-width stop buffer; must be non-negative.
- `rearmBufferRatio` (default `0.2`): zone-width distance required to arm from above or below; must be non-negative.
- `maxConsecutiveStops` (default `2`): terminal disable threshold for one zone; must be at least one.

`STOPPED` and `LIQUIDATED` increase the consecutive-stop count; `TAKE_PROFIT` resets it. `CLOSED`, rejected entries, and no-trade bars do not reset it. Reaching `maxConsecutiveStops` emits `ZONE_DISABLED`, which is terminal for that zone in the backtest.
