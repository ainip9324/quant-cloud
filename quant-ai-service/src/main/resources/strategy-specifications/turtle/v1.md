# Turtle v1

- Minimum data is 21 bars. Entry checks a 20-bar channel: close above the prior highest high opens LONG; close below the prior lowest low opens SHORT. If both conditions were somehow true on one bar, it holds.
- ATR period is 20. Risk per trade is fixed at `0.01` of cash using risk-per-price-unit sizing and a `2 × ATR` risk distance.
- LONG exits first on dynamic stop `entryPrice - 2 × ATR`, then on close below the 10-bar exit-channel low; SHORT mirrors this with `entryPrice + 2 × ATR` and a break above the exit-channel high.
- Only after exit checks, it can add in the favorable direction every `0.5 × ATR`, while `entryCount < 4`. Additions do not move the stop reference from the first entry price.
- The strategy is long/short mirrored and uses no fixed take-profit target. A consequence of the fixed first-entry stop is that total loss after additions can exceed the original one-unit risk estimate.
